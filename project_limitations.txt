PROJECT LIMITATIONS

1. The initial system will recognize only a predefined set of sign language gestures.

2. Recognition accuracy may vary depending on lighting conditions.

3. Complex gestures and continuous sign language sentences may not be supported in the initial version.

4. Background objects and multiple hands may affect recognition accuracy.

5. The system may require further training with diverse datasets to improve generalization.

6. The initial version may focus on static gestures rather than complete dynamic sign language sequences.
